# Healthcare AI (HAI) Lab

![HAI Lab](hai-lockup.png)

We are the **Healthcare AI (HAI)** Lab at the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Our research follows the human healthcare pipeline, building AI that supports clinicians across diagnosis, intervention, and care.

## Research directions
- Multimodal clinical diagnosis
- Surgical intervention and assistance (Surgery Copilot)
- Medical foundation models
